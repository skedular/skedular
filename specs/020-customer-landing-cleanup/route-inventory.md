## Source Route Snapshot

- App route files were collected from `src/web/apps/webapp/src/app/**/page.tsx` and `src/web/apps/webapp/src/app/**/route.ts`.
- Marketplace wrappers were collected from `src/web/apps/webapp/src/rootPages/marketplace/**`.
- Initial snapshot found root, marketplace, owner-specific marketplace, organization admin, MS Teams admin, auth, callback, notification, upload, integration, and places API surfaces.
- Current webapp build emits only customer marketplace, owner-specific marketplace, shared account/auth, Slack install, GraphQL/API upload/places, and customer-safe unsupported routes; `/organizations/**` and `/msteams/**` app routes are no longer emitted.

## Next Inventory Work

- Stakeholder approval is not final yet; records marked `reviewed` are implementation-ready for tests and route ownership constants, while `blocked` and `defer` records must not be removed.
- Customer-owned data risks are marked for marketplace bookings, subscriptions, invoice/payment/refund-adjacent paths, account state, users, and historical purchase links.
- Removed, relocated, owner-specific, and unsupported routes use `serve-in-place`, `unavailable-in-place`, `preserve-existing`, or `not-applicable`; no redirect handling is approved.
- Before route removal work, product and engineering should promote affected records from `reviewed` to `approved` or leave them blocked/deferred.
