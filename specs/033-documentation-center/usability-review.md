# Documentation Center Guided Usability Review

Use this record to complete T050 with at least 10 readers who are new to the product they evaluate. Do not count the implementation team as new readers.

## Facilitator script

1. Open `/docs`. Without prompting, ask the reader to choose the product that fits their work and open its Getting Started guide. Record whether they finish within 30 seconds.
2. After the reader finishes the guide, ask: “What would you do next?” Record whether they identify a relevant next setup action without help.
3. Ask the reader to use keyboard navigation to return to the product area and open a related article. Record any navigation, label, focus, or reading-order issue.
