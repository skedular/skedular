# Teams Glossary

These terms are written from the private organization point of view.

## Private Organization

A private organization is a company or internal workspace using Skedular for its own people.

## Admin

An admin is a user who can manage organization setup. Admin access should be limited to trusted people.

## Member

A member is a person who belongs to the private organization.

## Team

A team is a group of members inside the organization.

## Private Location

A private location is an internal workplace location. It is not a public marketplace listing.

## Resource

A resource is something members can reserve. It may be a desk, room, equipment item, or other shared thing.

## Private Booking

A private booking reserves a location or resource for internal use.

## Recurring Booking

A recurring booking repeats over time. Review recurrence carefully before changing it.

## Zone

A zone is an area inside a location. Zones help organize floor plans and resources.

## Floor Plan

A floor plan is a visual layout for a location.

## Availability Dashboard

The availability dashboard helps admins understand capacity and resource usage.

## SSO

SSO lets the organization use its identity provider for sign-in.

## Integration

An integration connects Skedular to another tool, such as Slack or Microsoft Teams.
