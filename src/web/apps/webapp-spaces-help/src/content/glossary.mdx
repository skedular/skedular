# Spaces Glossary

These terms are written from the marketplace and co-working operator point of view.

## Marketplace Organization

A marketplace organization is an operator account that can publish spaces, products, bookings, and subscriptions for customers.

## Operator

An operator is the person or business managing the marketplace side of Skedular.

## Marketplace Location

A marketplace location is a place customers may browse or book from.

## Resource

A resource is a bookable item or space inside a location.

## Product

A product is the public offer customers can book or subscribe to.

## One-Time Booking

A one-time booking is a customer reservation for a specific date or time.

## Subscription

A subscription is recurring customer access to a product.

## Refund

A refund is a financial workflow. It may follow cancellation, but it is a separate decision.

## Payment Setup

Payment setup is the operator-side configuration needed to accept or manage payments.

## Bank Account

A bank account page supports operator payment setup where available.

## Stripe Connect Account

A Stripe Connect account page supports provider-connected payment setup where available.

## Availability

Availability tells whether locations and resources can support bookings or subscriptions.

## Marketplace Setup

Marketplace setup is the group of steps that prepares the organization to sell to customers.

## Integration

An integration connects Skedular to another tool, such as Slack or Microsoft Teams.
