# Spaces Help Overview

Spaces Help explains the marketplace and co-working operator side of Skedular.

This is the right help site when your organization sells access to spaces, manages marketplace bookings, publishes products, reviews subscriptions, handles refund work, or connects payment and collaboration tools.

## What You Can Do

- Create or enter a marketplace organization.
- Set up the public marketplace side of the organization.
- Add locations, resources, zones, and floor plans.
- Create and manage marketplace products.
- Review operator bookings and subscriptions.
- Track customer records from the operator side.
- Review refund work at a public-safe level.
- Set up bank account or Stripe Connect account pages when available.
- Use analytics, availability, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and SSO pages.

## What Spaces Help Does Not Cover

Spaces Help does not explain customer self-service or private workplace team management.

Use Customer Help when you are booking a product or managing your own customer booking.

Use Teams Help when you manage private organization locations, private bookings, teams, members, and internal analytics.

## Main Areas

- **Marketplace setup:** Entry, admin pages, marketplace setup, public settings, sign-in, and welcome flows.
- **Locations and resources:** Operator locations, resources, zones, floor plans, and availability.
- **Commerce operations:** Products, bookings, subscriptions, refunds, and customer records.
- **Analytics and payments:** Analytics, availability, bank accounts, Stripe Connect, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and SSO.
- **Page reference:** What every main Spaces page is for and what to check there.
- **Operator examples:** Practical examples for locations, products, bookings, subscriptions, refunds, and payment setup.
- **Payment and refund safety:** Public-safe guidance for sensitive operator workflows.
- **Troubleshooting:** Common marketplace operator problems and what to check first.
- **Glossary:** Operator-friendly definitions for common terms.
- **Review checklists:** Quick checks before changing marketplace setup, products, bookings, refunds, or payments.
- **FAQ:** Short answers to common Spaces questions.
