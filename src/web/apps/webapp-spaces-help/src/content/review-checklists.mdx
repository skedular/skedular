# Spaces Review Checklists

Use these checklists before changing marketplace operator setup.

## Before Creating A Marketplace Organization

- I know this is marketplace or co-working operator work.
- I know who should administer the organization.
- I know the public organization name.
- I know whether payments will be needed.
- I know which locations will be published.

## Before Publishing A Location

- The location name is customer-friendly.
- The address or public place detail is correct.
- Resources are connected.
- Floor plan or zone information is clear when used.
- The location should be public.
- Support expectations are understood.

## Before Creating A Product

- The product name is clear.
- The product is connected to the right location or resource.
- I know whether it is one-time, recurring, or both.
- Availability rules are understood.
- Customer-facing policy notes are ready.
- Payment setup is ready if required.

## Before Reviewing A Booking

- I opened the correct organization.
- I found the correct customer record.
- I checked the product.
- I checked the location and resource.
- I checked date, time, and status.
- I checked visible payment or fulfillment signals.

## Before Reviewing A Subscription

- I checked the product.
- I checked the recurring schedule.
- I checked current status.
- I checked related bookings when shown.
- I checked cancellation or renewal information.
- I know whether the issue affects future periods.

## Before Handling Refund Work

- I opened the related booking or subscription.
- I checked the visible payment state.
- I checked the product policy.
- I know whether cancellation has happened.
- I know whether manual review is needed.
- I removed sensitive data from any screenshots.

## Before Changing Payment Setup

- The authorized payment owner is available.
- The organization context is correct.
- Provider credentials are not being shared.
- Bank details are not visible in public screenshots.
- The change is approved internally.
