# Spaces Help

Spaces Help is for co-working and marketplace operators who use Skedular to publish spaces, manage products, run bookings, review subscriptions, and keep payments and integrations working.

Use this help site when you are running the marketplace side of Skedular. That includes setting up a marketplace organization, adding locations, publishing products, checking operator bookings, reviewing refunds, and connecting payment or collaboration tools.

## Start Here

- **Set up your marketplace:** Learn how marketplace organizations, setup pages, and public marketplace settings work.
- **Prepare places to sell:** Understand locations, resources, zones, floor plans, and availability.
- **Run commerce:** Learn about products, bookings, subscriptions, refunds, and customer records from the operator side.
- **Connect operations:** Learn how analytics, availability, payments, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and SSO fit together.

## Use Another Help Site When

- You are a customer booking a space or managing your own booking. Use Customer Help.
- You manage private company workspaces, internal teams, and private bookings. Use Teams Help.
