# Users

## Overview

Users represent the people who belong to an organization and interact with its resources.

Depending on the Skedular product being used, users can manage an organization, create bookings, administer locations, or simply book resources for themselves.

A user's permissions are determined by the role assigned to them within an organization.

Users are always members of an organization, and the same person can belong to multiple organizations with different roles in each.

---

# Product Support

User management differs slightly across the Skedular products.

| Product             | User Support                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Skedular Teams**  | Full user management with Owners, Administrators, and Members.                                     |
| **Skedular Spaces** | Full user management for managing coworking spaces and marketplace organizations.                  |
| **Skedular Host**   | Currently supports a single owner only. Additional organization users are not currently supported. |

---

# Roles

Each user is assigned one or more roles within an organization.

Roles determine what actions the user is allowed to perform.

Typical roles include:

## Owner

The owner has full control over the organization.

Owners can:

- Manage organization settings.
- Manage billing and subscriptions.
- Create and manage locations.
- Manage resources.
- Invite and remove users.
- Manage teams.
- Configure tags and zones.
- View analytics.
- Manage bookings.
- Assign user roles.

Every organization has at least one owner.

---

## Administrator

Administrators help manage the organization.

Typical responsibilities include:

- Managing locations.
- Managing resources.
- Managing bookings.
- Managing teams.
- Inviting users.
- Configuring operational settings.

Administrators usually have access to most management features but may not have permission to manage billing or ownership settings.

---

## Member

Members are standard users of the organization.

Depending on the organization's configuration, members may be able to:

- Create bookings.
- Manage their own bookings.
- View resource availability.
- Join teams.
- View other members' bookings, where permitted.

Members generally do not have access to administrative functions.

---

# Users in Skedular Teams

In **Skedular Teams**, users represent employees or members of a private organization.

Users can:

- Book desks.
- Book meeting rooms.
- Book parking spaces.
- Join teams.
- View availability.
- Participate in team bookings.

Depending on the organization's privacy settings, members may also be able to see bookings made by other members.

This helps support hybrid work planning and office collaboration.

---

# Users in Skedular Spaces

In **Skedular Spaces**, users are responsible for operating the coworking business.

Typical users include:

- Owners.
- Administrators.
- Workspace managers.
- Reception staff.

These users manage:

- Locations.
- Resources.
- Customers.
- Bookings.
- Products.
- Pricing.
- Analytics.
- Payments.

Unlike private organizations, customer booking details are generally visible only to organization owners and administrators.

This helps protect customer privacy.

---

# Users in Skedular Host

**Skedular Host** currently uses a simplified model.

Each host organization has a single owner who manages the entire organization.

Additional organization users are not currently supported.

The owner manages:

- Locations.
- Resources.
- Bookings.
- Availability.
- Organization settings.

Support for additional users may be introduced in future releases.

---

# Team Membership

In **Skedular Teams**, users can be assigned to one or more teams.

Teams allow organizations to:

- Organise employees.
- Simplify group bookings.
- Coordinate office attendance.

Only users who belong to the organization can become members of its teams.

---

# Booking Permissions

Users can create bookings based on the permissions assigned to their role.

Depending on the organization, users may be able to:

- Book resources for themselves.
- Book resources on behalf of other users.
- Create bookings for entire teams.
- Modify bookings.
- Cancel bookings.

Higher-level roles typically have broader booking permissions.

---

# Booking Visibility

Who can view booking information depends on the organization type.

## Private Organizations

Members can typically view bookings made by other members, subject to the organization's privacy settings.

This helps employees understand who will be working from the office and which resources are already in use.

---

## Marketplace Organizations

Customer privacy is prioritised.

Only organization owners and administrators generally have access to detailed booking information.

Customers can view only their own bookings.

---

## Host Organizations

The organization owner can manage all bookings for their locations.

Customers can view only their own bookings.

---

# Managing Users

Depending on their permissions, administrators can:

- Invite users.
- Remove users.
- Assign roles.
- Change permissions.
- Manage team membership.

User management is performed from within the organization.

---

# Things to Know

- Users always belong to an organization.
- A person can belong to multiple organizations.
- Users can have different roles in different organizations.
- Roles determine what actions users can perform.
- Team membership is available only in **Skedular Teams**.
- **Skedular Host** currently supports a single owner only.
- Booking visibility depends on the organization's privacy settings.

---

# Best Practices

For the best experience:

- Assign users the minimum permissions they require.
- Regularly review user roles.
- Remove users who no longer require access.
- Keep team membership up to date.
- Use administrators to delegate day-to-day management.
