# Teams

## Overview

Teams allow organizations to group members who regularly work, collaborate, or attend the office together.

Instead of creating bookings for individual users one at a time, administrators and authorized members can create a single booking for an entire team.

Teams are currently available only in **Skedular Teams**, where they are designed to simplify workplace booking for private organizations.

---

# Availability

The Teams feature is currently supported only in **private organizations** using **Skedular Teams**.

It is not currently available in:

- Skedular Spaces
- Skedular Host

These products focus on customer and resource management rather than internal workforce planning.

---

# Why Use Teams?

Teams make it easy to coordinate office attendance for groups of people.

Instead of selecting every employee individually, a booking can be created for the entire team in a single action.

This saves time and helps organizations manage hybrid working arrangements more efficiently.

Common scenarios include:

- Bringing an entire department into the office.
- Booking desks for a project team.
- Reserving meeting rooms for a working group.
- Coordinating office attendance on specific days.
- Planning regular in-office collaboration.

---

# How Teams Work

A team belongs to a single organization and contains one or more members.

Each team can include:

- Employees
- Members
- Managers
- Team leads

When a booking is created for a team, Skedular automatically creates bookings for each member of that team, subject to resource availability and the organization's booking rules.

This eliminates the need to create individual bookings for every team member.

---

# Team Members

Each team consists of users who belong to the same organization.

A user may:

- Belong to no teams.
- Belong to one team.
- Belong to multiple teams.

This flexibility allows organizations to model departments, project groups, committees, or any other logical grouping of people.

---

# Creating a Team

To create a team:

1. Open **Teams**.
2. Select **Create Team**.
3. Enter a team name.
4. Add members to the team.
5. Save the team.

The team is immediately available for future bookings.

---

# Managing Teams

Authorized users can:

- Create teams.
- Edit team details.
- Add or remove members.
- Rename teams.
- Delete teams when they are no longer required.

Changes to team membership affect future bookings but do not modify bookings that have already been created.

---

# Team Bookings

One of the primary purposes of teams is simplifying bookings.

Instead of selecting every user individually, you can:

1. Create a booking.
2. Select a team.
3. Choose the required resources.
4. Select the booking date and time.
5. Confirm the booking.

Skedular handles the booking process for the members of the selected team.

This makes scheduling significantly faster for larger groups.

---

# Team Visibility

Teams are managed within the organization.

Depending on the organization's permission settings, members may be able to:

- View teams.
- View team members.
- Create team bookings.
- Manage team membership.

Organization owners and administrators always have full access to team management.

---

# Best Practices

To get the most value from teams:

- Create teams that reflect how people actually work together.
- Keep team membership up to date.
- Remove inactive members from teams.
- Use descriptive team names.
- Review teams periodically as your organization changes.

---

# Things to Know

- Teams are available only in **Skedular Teams**.
- Teams belong to a single organization.
- Teams contain one or more users.
- Users can belong to multiple teams.
- Teams simplify booking resources for groups of people.
- Updating a team's membership affects future bookings only.
- Organization owners and administrators can manage teams.

---

# Example

An organization has a team called **Engineering** with ten members.

Rather than creating ten separate desk bookings, an administrator creates one booking for the **Engineering** team.

Skedular automatically creates the required bookings for each team member, making it quick and easy to coordinate office attendance.
