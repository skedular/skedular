# Billing and Payouts

## Overview

The **Billing and Payouts** section allows marketplace organizations to configure how customers are billed, how payments are collected, and where funds are paid.

This section is available at the organization level and is used to configure the commercial and financial settings for **Skedular Spaces**.

These settings apply across all products, subscriptions, and customer purchases within the organization.

---

# Availability

Billing and Payouts is currently available only in:

- **Skedular Spaces**

It is not available in:

- Skedular Teams
- Skedular Host

Only organization owners and administrators can access and modify these settings.

---

# Why Billing and Payouts Exists

Billing and Payouts centralises all financial configuration for the organization.

It allows administrators to:

- Configure billing schedules.
- Connect accounting systems.
- Configure payment providers.
- Manage bank accounts.
- Choose accepted payment methods.

Having these settings at the organization level ensures consistent billing behaviour across every product and subscription.

---

# Accessing Billing and Payouts

To configure billing settings:

1. Open your organization.
2. Navigate to **Admin**.
3. Select **Billing and Payouts**.

From here you can manage every financial integration and billing configuration for the organization.

---

# Billing Cadence

Billing cadence determines how frequently invoices are generated for customers using billing in arrears.

Supported billing schedules include:

- Weekly
- Fortnightly
- Monthly

The selected cadence is applied wherever billing in arrears is used.

Choosing the correct billing cadence helps align invoicing with your business operations.

---

# Payment Methods

Organizations can configure which payment methods customers are allowed to use.

Depending on the organization's configuration, supported payment methods may include:

- Credit or debit card
- Bank transfer

Payment methods can then be enabled or disabled for individual product offers.

---

# Stripe Integration

Organizations can connect their Stripe account to accept online card payments.

Stripe integration enables:

- Secure online payments.
- Automatic payment confirmation.
- Faster checkout for customers.
- Automatic payment processing.

Once connected, supported offers can accept card payments directly through Stripe.

---

# Xero Integration

Organizations can connect Xero to automate accounting processes.

The integration allows Skedular to work with your accounting workflow by supporting activities such as:

- Invoice creation.
- Customer synchronisation.
- Payment reconciliation.

This reduces manual administration and helps keep accounting records up to date.

---

# Bank Accounts

Organizations can configure one or more bank accounts for direct bank transfer payments.

Bank account information is used when customers choose to pay by bank transfer.

This allows organizations to support manual payment workflows alongside online payments.

---

# Payment Processing

Payment processing is determined by the configuration of each product offer together with the organization's billing settings.

Depending on the offer, customers may pay using:

- Stripe
- Bank transfer
- Other supported payment methods

The organization controls which payment methods are available.

---

# Billing in the Booking Process

Billing and payment settings are used whenever customers purchase:

- Products
- Recurring subscriptions
- Marketplace bookings

These settings work together with each product offer to determine:

- How customers pay.
- When invoices are generated.
- When payments are collected.
- How recurring billing is managed.

---

# Managing Billing Settings

Organization owners and administrators can:

- Change the billing cadence.
- Connect or disconnect Stripe.
- Connect or disconnect Xero.
- Update bank account information.
- Configure accepted payment methods.

Changes are applied across the organization.

---

# Best Practices

For the best experience:

- Configure billing cadence before publishing products.
- Keep accounting integrations connected and up to date.
- Verify bank account details regularly.
- Enable only the payment methods you intend to support.
- Review payment settings whenever introducing new commercial offerings.

---

# Things to Know

- Billing and Payouts is configured at the organization level.
- It is available only in **Skedular Spaces**.
- Billing cadence supports weekly, fortnightly, and monthly schedules.
- Stripe can be connected for online card payments.
- Xero can be connected for accounting integration.
- Bank accounts can be configured for bank transfer payments.
- Payment methods are managed centrally and used throughout the organization.

---

# Example

A coworking operator configures the organization's billing settings as follows:

- Billing cadence: Monthly
- Stripe: Connected
- Xero: Connected
- Bank account: Configured for direct transfers
- Accepted payment methods:
  - Credit Card
  - Bank Transfer

The operator then creates products that allow customers to pay either by card or bank transfer.

Recurring memberships are billed monthly, invoices are synchronised with Xero, and card payments are automatically processed through Stripe.
