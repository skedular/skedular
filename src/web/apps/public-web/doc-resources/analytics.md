# Analytics

## Overview

Analytics provides insights into how your organization, locations, and resources are being used over time.

It helps administrators understand booking trends, resource utilisation, occupancy, and workplace activity so they can make informed operational decisions.

Analytics is available in:

- **Skedular Teams**
- **Skedular Spaces**
- **Skedular Host**

All analytics are generated automatically from booking and resource activity.

---

# Why Use Analytics?

Analytics helps organizations answer questions such as:

- When are our busiest days?
- Which locations are used the most?
- Which resources are underutilised?
- What are our peak booking periods?
- How busy is each office?
- Are we making the best use of our available resources?

By understanding these trends, organizations can improve space planning, staffing, and resource allocation.

---

# Accessing Analytics

Analytics is available at the organization level.

To view analytics:

1. Open your organization.
2. Navigate to **Analytics**.
3. Select the type of insight you want to view.
4. Choose the reporting period.

Analytics can be viewed for the entire organization or for individual locations.

---

# Reporting Periods

Analytics can be viewed over different time ranges.

Available reporting periods include:

- Past Week
- Past Month
- Past 3 Months
- Past 6 Months
- Custom Date Range

This allows organizations to compare short-term activity with longer-term trends.

---

# Booking Insights

Booking Insights shows how bookings change over time.

These reports help identify:

- Peak booking days.
- Quiet periods.
- Booking trends.
- Overall booking volume.

Organizations can use this information to better understand how their workspaces are being used.

---

# Desk Occupancy Insights

Desk Occupancy Insights measures how effectively desks are being utilised.

Typical metrics include:

- Occupancy percentage.
- Number of occupied desks.
- Capacity utilisation.
- Historical occupancy trends.

This helps organizations determine whether they have the right number of desks available for their workforce.

---

# Resource Availability Insights

Resource Availability Insights provides an overview of the current state of resources.

Typical resource states include:

- Available
- Booked
- Unavailable

These reports help administrators understand how much of their resource inventory is actively being used.

---

# Location Analytics

Organizations with multiple locations can analyse each location independently.

Location-level reporting helps compare:

- Booking activity.
- Occupancy.
- Resource utilisation.
- Capacity.
- Availability.

This makes it easier to identify which locations are operating efficiently and which may require changes.

---

# Organization Analytics

Organization-level analytics combine data across all locations.

These reports provide a high-level view of workplace activity, helping administrators understand overall performance without reviewing each location individually.

---

# Trend Analysis

Analytics is designed to highlight trends rather than individual bookings.

Examples include:

- Increasing office attendance.
- Seasonal booking patterns.
- Frequently used locations.
- Changes in workspace utilisation.
- Long-term occupancy trends.

Trend analysis helps organizations make strategic decisions based on real usage data.

---

# Automatic Updates

Analytics is generated automatically from activity within the booking system.

Reports are updated as bookings are:

- Created.
- Modified.
- Cancelled.

There is no manual reporting process required.

---

# Best Practices

To get the most value from Analytics:

- Review reports regularly.
- Compare different reporting periods.
- Monitor occupancy before expanding office space.
- Identify underutilised resources.
- Use historical trends to support future planning.
- Review each location independently when managing multiple sites.

---

# Things to Know

- Analytics is available at the organization level.
- Reports can be viewed for the entire organization or individual locations.
- Multiple reporting periods are supported.
- Booking Insights show booking activity over time.
- Desk Occupancy Insights measure workspace utilisation.
- Resource Availability Insights show the current state of resources.
- Reports update automatically as booking activity changes.

---

# Example

A company manages offices in Auckland, Wellington, and Christchurch.

The Workplace Manager reviews the previous three months of analytics.

The reports show:

- Auckland consistently reaches over 90% desk occupancy on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.
- Wellington averages around 50% occupancy throughout the week.
- Christchurch has significantly lower booking activity than expected.

Based on these insights, the company decides to reorganise its office attendance policy and reduce unused workspace in Christchurch while preparing additional desks for Auckland's busiest days.
