# Floor Plans

## Overview

Floor plans provide a visual representation of a location and show where resources are physically positioned.

They help users quickly understand the layout of a workplace, making it easier to find and book the right resource.

Floor plans are managed as part of a location and are available in:

- **Skedular Teams**
- **Skedular Spaces**
- **Skedular Host**

A location can have no floor plans, a single floor plan, or multiple floor plans.

---

# Why Use Floor Plans?

While resources can always be booked from a list, floor plans provide a much more intuitive experience.

They allow users to:

- See where resources are located.
- Understand the physical layout of a workplace.
- Find colleagues sitting nearby.
- Select desks based on their preferred location.
- View resource availability visually.
- Navigate large offices or venues more easily.

Floor plans make booking resources faster and more natural, especially for workplaces with many desks or rooms.

---

# How Floor Plans Work

Floor plans belong to a single location.

Each location may contain multiple floor plans representing different areas of the site.

Examples include:

- Ground Floor
- Level 1
- Level 2
- East Wing
- West Wing
- Building A
- Building B

Each floor plan contains a visual layout together with the resources positioned on it.

---

# Uploading a Floor Plan

Administrators upload an image or drawing of the physical space to create a floor plan.

Once uploaded, the floor plan becomes the canvas used to position resources.

The image should accurately represent the physical layout of the location.

---

# Placing Resources

After creating a floor plan, administrators position resources on the map.

Resources can include:

- Desks
- Meeting rooms
- Parking spaces
- Offices
- Equipment
- Other resource types

Each resource is placed at its real-world position, allowing users to easily identify where it is located.

---

# Resource Assignment

A resource can either:

- Not appear on any floor plan.
- Be assigned to a single floor plan.

A resource **cannot** appear on multiple floor plans at the same time.

If a resource is already assigned to one floor plan, it must first be removed from that floor plan before it can be assigned to another.

This ensures every resource has a single, accurate physical location.

---

# Viewing Availability

Floor plans display resource availability for a selected date.

Depending on the current bookings, users can quickly identify which resources are:

- Available
- Booked
- Unavailable

This allows users to choose suitable resources directly from the map without searching through long lists.

---

# Finding Colleagues

In **Skedular Teams**, floor plans also help users understand where people are working.

Depending on the organization's privacy settings, members can see which desks have been booked and who is sitting nearby.

This supports hybrid work by making it easier for colleagues to work together in the office.

---

# Managing Floor Plans

Floor plans are managed from within a location.

Administrators can:

- Create floor plans.
- Upload floor plan images.
- Rename floor plans.
- Position resources.
- Move resources.
- Remove resources.
- Delete floor plans.

Floor plans can be updated at any time as office layouts change.

---

# Best Practices

For the best experience:

- Create one floor plan for each physical level or area.
- Use clear and up-to-date floor plan images.
- Position resources accurately.
- Remove resources that no longer exist.
- Update floor plans whenever the physical layout changes.

---

# Things to Know

- Floor plans belong to a location.
- A location can contain multiple floor plans.
- Floor plans are optional.
- Resources may be positioned on a floor plan.
- A resource can belong to only one floor plan at a time.
- Resources must be removed from their current floor plan before being assigned to another.
- Floor plans provide a visual booking experience.
- Availability is displayed directly on the floor plan.

---

# Example

An office has three levels.

The administrator creates three floor plans:

- Ground Floor
- Level One
- Level Two

Each desk, meeting room, and collaboration space is positioned on the appropriate floor plan.

When employees book a desk, they can select a date, open the relevant floor plan, see which desks are available, view where their colleagues are sitting (subject to privacy settings), and choose the desk that best suits their needs.
