# Credit-based booking entitlements

Product pricing can grant a fixed number of booking credits for a defined validity period. Purchasing credits creates no Booking or Resource allocation; each credit later represents one qualifying booking. A pricing option may limit credit spending to selected calendar days; leave the selection empty to allow every day. Stripe card payment follows the automatic checkout path, while bank-transfer payment remains pending until an authorized operator confirms it. Credits are granted only after payment is confirmed. Customers and authorized operators can create, modify, and cancel credit-funded Bookings. Auto-renewal uses the same Stripe or manual bank-transfer payment path, re-evaluates the current active credit pricing, and grants the next cycle only after confirmation. A failed renewal does not extend the current cycle or grant unconfirmed credits. The pricing option captures whether unused credits may be refunded. Confirmed Stripe refunds may be automatic; bank-transfer refunds are manual; Xero creates a credit note while settlement remains manual. Reservation-based and recurring booking behavior remains unchanged; credits are an additional fulfillment path for later bookings.
