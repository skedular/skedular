# Zones

## Overview

Zones are organization-wide labels that let you group resources based on their physical location, purpose, or any other logical grouping that makes sense for your organization.

Zones make it easier to organize resources, improve filtering, and create a better booking experience for your users.

Examples of zones include:

- First Floor
- Ground Floor
- Engineering
- Sales
- Window Side
- Quiet Area
- East Wing
- Building A

A zone can represent either a physical area or a logical grouping. Your organization is free to define zones that match the way your workplace operates.

---

## How Zones Work

Zones are created once at the organization level and are shared across the entire organization.

After a zone has been created, it can be assigned to one or more resources.

Each resource can belong to:

- No zones
- One zone
- Multiple zones

This flexibility allows resources to be categorized in multiple ways.

For example:

A meeting room may belong to:

- First Floor
- Engineering
- Window Side

while another desk may simply belong to:

- Sales

---

## Why Use Zones?

Zones help organizations organise their resources without changing the physical structure of locations.

Common uses include:

- Grouping desks by department
- Identifying resources on a particular floor
- Grouping resources by building
- Creating quiet or collaboration areas
- Organising parking spaces
- Categorising equipment
- Improving search and filtering
- Helping users quickly find suitable resources

---

## Managing Zones

Only users with the appropriate administrative permissions can manage zones.

Zones are managed from:

**Admin → Zones**

From this page administrators can:

- View all existing zones
- Create a new zone
- Edit an existing zone
- Delete a zone

Changes made to a zone become available throughout the organization immediately.

---

## Creating a Zone

To create a new zone:

1. Open **Admin**.
2. Select **Zones**.
3. Click **Add Zone**.
4. Enter a unique zone name.
5. Save the zone.

The new zone is now available to be assigned to resources.

---

## Editing a Zone

To rename or update a zone:

1. Open **Admin**.
2. Select **Zones**.
3. Select the zone you wish to edit.
4. Update the zone name.
5. Save your changes.

Any resources already assigned to the zone will automatically use the updated name.

---

## Deleting a Zone

If a zone is no longer required:

1. Open **Admin**.
2. Select **Zones**.
3. Choose the zone.
4. Delete the zone.

Deleting a zone removes the zone definition from the organization.

Resources that previously referenced the zone will no longer be associated with it.

---

## Assigning Zones to Resources

Zones themselves do not contain resources.

Instead, resources reference one or more zones.

When editing a resource, administrators can select any combination of available zones.

For example:

| Resource       | Assigned Zones           |
| -------------- | ------------------------ |
| Desk A12       | First Floor, Engineering |
| Meeting Room 1 | First Floor, Window Side |
| Parking Bay 8  | Basement                 |
| Desk B04       | Sales                    |

---

## Best Practices

For the best experience:

- Keep zone names short and descriptive.
- Use consistent naming across your organization.
- Avoid creating duplicate zones with similar meanings.
- Use zones to describe groups rather than individual resources.
- Review unused zones periodically and remove them if they are no longer required.

---

## Things to Know

- Zones are created at the organization level.
- All locations within the organization can use the same zones.
- A resource may belong to multiple zones.
- Resources are not required to belong to any zone.
- Renaming a zone updates it everywhere it is used.
- Deleting a zone removes the association from all resources.
