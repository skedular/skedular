Find quick answers to common questions about setting up and using Skedular Teams. For step-by-step instructions, follow the links to the relevant guides.

## Do I need to create a Team before I can make a Booking?

No. Teams are optional. You can make a Booking without creating a Team; a Team can be associated with a Booking when that helps organize the workplace. See [Organize your people](/docs/teams/workplace-setup/organize-your-people).

## Can I create a Booking for another person?

Yes, an active Organization member with permission to add Bookings can create a Booking for another Organization member. For the complete Booking workflow and visibility rules, see [Bookings](/docs/teams/bookings).

## Why can't I see a Resource when adding a Booking?

The Resource may already be booked, fall outside the selected Location or available hours, or not be available to you. Review [Set up your workplace](/docs/teams/workplace-setup/set-up-your-workplace) and [Bookings](/docs/teams/bookings).

## Can a Booking be associated with a Team?

Yes. A Booking can include a Team association, but associating a Team does not create a Booking for every Team member. Learn how people and Teams are organized in [Organize your people](/docs/teams/workplace-setup/organize-your-people).

## Can I use Skedular Teams from Slack?

Yes. The Slack app supports workplace Booking and Location workflows, including daily **Who's in today?** updates. See the [Slack integration guide](/docs/teams/integrations/slack) for setup and usage details.

## What does `Join` do in the Slack app?

**Join** creates a separate private Booking for you using the shared Booking's time and Team association. It does not add you to the original Booking or assign you its Resources. See [Slack integration](/docs/teams/integrations/slack).

## Can my Organization use SSO?

Yes. SSO is configured for one Organization by an active Owner or Administrator. Members must already be active Organization members; enabling SSO does not automatically add new members. See [Enterprise sign-in](/docs/teams/integrations/enterprise-sign-in).

## Where should I configure Locations, Resources, and members?

Use the Skedular Teams web app to set up your workplace. Start with [Set up your workplace](/docs/teams/workplace-setup/set-up-your-workplace), then use [Organize your workplace](/docs/teams/workplace-setup/organize-your-workplace) and [Organize your people](/docs/teams/workplace-setup/organize-your-people).
