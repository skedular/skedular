Your place is the central part of Skedular Host. You rent the place as a whole rather than configuring individual desks, rooms, or other workspace resources. The information here helps renters understand what the place is, where it is, what it offers, and whether it suits their needs.

The place becomes available online when it is created, so keep the renter-facing information accurate from the beginning and review it before sharing the listing.

## Describe your place

Use the supported place details to explain what renters are booking. Keep the Name, Timezone, Space Type, people capacity, and Contact Details accurate. Straightforward information helps renters understand the real offering without relying on claims the place cannot support.

## Set the location

Enter the place address and confirm the map location shown by Host. Accurate location information helps renters decide whether to book and plan their visit. Review it whenever the address or map location changes.

## Add images

Upload Feature Images for the place, remove images that are no longer accurate, and use Make cover to choose the image that represents it in discovery. You can keep multiple images when they show the place clearly. Use recent images, and do not add anything that could give renters a misleading impression.

## Amenities

Keep the Amenities shown for the place accurate. These details tell renters what is included with a booking, so remove anything that is no longer available and add information that helps them assess whether the place meets their needs.

## Review what renters see

Whenever important place information changes, review the renter-facing listing. Confirm that the place details match reality, the address is correct, the images are current, and the Amenities are complete. Configure pricing, availability, booking conditions, and cancellation policies separately in [Pricing and availability](/docs/host/pricing-and-availability).

## Next step

Once the place information is accurate, continue to [Pricing and availability](/docs/host/pricing-and-availability) to configure when the place can be booked, how much it costs, and the relevant booking conditions.
