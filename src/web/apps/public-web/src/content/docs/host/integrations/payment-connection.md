## What this feature is for

**Payment Connection** is part of Skedular Host's integrations workflow. Use it to keep the relevant work visible, consistent, and easy to hand off to the next person.

## How it fits the product

In Host, this capability belongs to the integrations area. Teams coordinates private workplaces, Spaces supports commercial workspace operations, and Host keeps independent place rental focused on a single place.

## What to check before you use it

Confirm that you are working in the correct organization and have the access needed for this area. Review the location, people, availability, or payment context that applies to the feature before making a change. Keep customer records, credentials, provider internals, and account-specific values inside the product rather than in public documentation.
