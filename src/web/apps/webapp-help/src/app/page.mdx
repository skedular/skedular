# Customer Help

Customer Help is for people who use Skedular to find a place, book a marketplace product, manage a booking, or manage a recurring subscription.

Use this help site when you are acting as a customer. That usually means you are browsing public locations, choosing a product, checking your booking details, or looking for what you can do next.

## Start Here

- **Find a place:** Learn how marketplace locations, public organization pages, and floor plans work.
- **Choose a product:** Understand one-time bookings, recurring subscriptions, availability, and product rules.
- **Manage your bookings:** See where to find booking details, subscription details, cancellation options, and refund guidance.
- **Manage your account:** Learn about notifications, settings, sign-in, welcome pages, and unsupported links.

## Use Another Help Site When

- You manage private company locations, teams, people, or internal bookings. Use Teams Help.
- You run a marketplace or co-working space and need to publish products, manage payments, or handle operator refunds. Use Spaces Help.
