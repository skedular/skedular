# Customer FAQ

## Is Customer Help Public?

Yes. Customer Help is written so anyone can read it without signing in. It avoids sensitive account, payment, and internal operator details.

## What Is The Customer App For?

It is for finding marketplace locations, choosing products, making bookings, starting subscriptions, and managing your own customer records.

## What Is A Product?

A product is something an operator offers. It might be a desk, room, day pass, membership, or another type of access.

## What Is A Booking?

A booking is a one-time reservation or a specific reserved time.

## What Is A Subscription?

A subscription is recurring access. It may renew or create related bookings depending on the product setup.

## Why Can I Not See A Cancellation Button?

The booking or subscription may not be eligible, the allowed time may have passed, payment may not be confirmed, or the operator may need to handle it.

## Does Cancellation Always Mean Refund?

No. Cancellation and refund are different. Cancellation affects access. Refund affects money. The refund outcome depends on policy, payment state, timing, and operator review.

## Why Is A Product Unavailable?

It may be fully booked, outside its allowed schedule, unpublished, unavailable to your account, or linked to a location or resource that is not available.

## Can Customer Help Explain Operator Setup?

No. Customer Help explains what customers see and do. Operator setup belongs in Spaces Help.

## Can Customer Help Explain Private Organization Bookings?

No. Private organization bookings belong in Teams Help.
