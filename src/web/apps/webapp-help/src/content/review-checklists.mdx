# Customer Review Checklists

Use these checklists when you want to avoid mistakes.

## Before Browsing

- I have the correct marketplace link.
- I know which operator or location I want.
- I am using Customer Help, not Teams or Spaces Help.

## Before Booking

- I checked the product name.
- I checked the location.
- I checked the date and time.
- I checked whether it is one-time or recurring.
- I read visible rules and policy notes.
- I know which account will own the booking.

## Before Subscribing

- I understand that the product is recurring.
- I checked the schedule or renewal information shown by the app.
- I read visible cancellation or policy notes.
- I know how to find the subscription later.

## Before Canceling

- I opened the correct booking or subscription detail page.
- I understand whether cancellation is immediate or future-only.
- I checked whether refund information is shown.
- I know the action may not always create a refund.

## Before Asking For Support

- I have the booking or subscription reference.
- I know the product name.
- I know the location.
- I know the date and time.
- I can explain what I expected.
- I removed sensitive information from screenshots.

## After A Change

- I refreshed the detail page once.
- I checked the status.
- I checked the booking or subscription list.
- I saved any confirmation reference shown by the app.
